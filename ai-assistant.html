7-day wind-down plan (start tonight)

  1. Day 1 — Fix a consistent wake-up time (even on weekends).
  2. Day 2 — Cut caffeine after 14:00; switch to water or herbal tea.
  3. Day 3 — Screens off 60 minutes before bed; dim household lights.
  4. Day 4 — Add a 10-minute wind-down: stretch + slow breathing (4-7-8).
  5. Day 5 — Move your last big meal 3 hours before bedtime.
  6. Day 6 — Bed = sleep only. If awake >20 minutes, get up and read something boring.
  7. Day 7 — Review: which habit moved the needle? Keep it for 30 days.

When to see a doctor

If insomnia persists more than 3 nights a week for 3 months, or you experience loud snoring with daytime sleepiness, please consult a qualified physician. Self-help guides like this one are not medical advice.
