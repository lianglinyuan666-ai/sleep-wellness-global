About Mian Jing

Blending Eastern wellness philosophy with modern sleep science to help you rest better, naturally.

Our Story

Mian Jing (眠境, meaning "Sleep Sanctuary") was born from a simple realization: sleep is the foundation of health, yet millions of people are quietly losing it to screens, stress, and modern life.

We combine traditional Eastern wellness practices with evidence-based sleep science — offering nature soundscapes, guided meditation, dietary wisdom, and curated sleep products. We don't make medical claims. We provide science-backed education and warm companionship.

Inspired by the minimalist aesthetics of thisworks, Manta Sleep, Bearaby, and SmartSleep, we focus on "light design, rich content" — creating a digital sanctuary where every visitor feels gently cared for.
