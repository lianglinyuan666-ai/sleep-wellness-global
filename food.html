Curated Recipes

8 Traditional Sleep-Nourishing Recipes

Herbal TeaClassic
Suan Zao Ren Tea (Ziziphus Jujuba)
Suan Zao Ren 15g · Fu Ling 10g · Bai Zi Ren 8g · Water 500ml
Crush Suan Zao Ren, simmer all ingredients for 20 min. Drink warm 1 hour before bed. The jujuboside compounds act as GABA-A receptor agonists.
🔬 IMA Sleep Breath Meditation: Jujuboside A shows sedative effects comparable to diazepam in animal models, without dependency risk. Confidence 7/10
Warm Porridge
Lily Bulb & Lotus Seed Porridge
Bai He 20g · Lian Zi 15g · Glutinous Rice 50g · Rock Sugar to taste
Soak lotus seeds 2hrs. Cook all ingredients in 800ml water on low heat for 40 min until creamy. Lily bulb (Bai He) clears heart-fire per TCM, reducing anxiety-driven insomnia.
🔬 IMA Good Sleep: Bai He contains steroidal saponins with demonstrated anxiolytic effects. Confidence 6/10
TeaQuick
Longan & Red Date Calming Tea
Dried Longan 8 pcs · Red Dates 5 pcs · Goji Berry 10g · Water 400ml
A 10-minute sweet brew. Longan (Gui Yuan) nourishes heart blood in TCM; red dates (Da Zao) tonify spleen Qi. Ideal for those with heart palpitations at bedtime.
🔬 IMA Sleep Breath Meditation: Longan extract demonstrates neuroprotective effects and mild sedative properties. Confidence 6/10
Classic Formula
Gan Mai Da Zao Decoction
Fu Xiao Mai 30g · Da Zao 10 pcs · Gan Cao 9g · Water 600ml
Zhang Zhongjing's classic formula from Shang Han Lun (200 CE). Simmer 25 min, drink throughout the evening. Designed for "Zang Zao" — visceral restlessness with emotional volatility.
🔬 IMA MCBT-I Sleep: Historical formula used for 1,800+ years. Modern trials suggest efficacy for menopausal sleep disturbance. Confidence 7/10
Night Drink
Cinnamon Turmeric Golden Milk
Milk 250ml · Turmeric 1tsp · Cinnamon stick 1 · Honey 1tsp · Black Pepper pinch
Warm milk with turmeric, cinnamon, and a pinch of black pepper (enhances curcumin absorption 2000%). The tryptophan in warm milk plus turmeric's anti-inflammatory effects promote rest.
🔬 IMA Good Sleep: Warm milk at bedtime is more than folklore — the combination of tryptophan + warmth + ritual creates a Pavlovian sleep cue. Confidence 8/10
Cooling Tea
Chrysanthemum & Goji Eye-Relax Tea
Chrysanthemum 5 flowers · Goji Berry 15g · Cassia Seed 10g · Water 400ml
Perfect for screen-tired eyes before bed. Chrysanthemum clears liver heat (TCM: excess screen time = liver yang rising). Cassia seed (Jue Ming Zi) is traditionally used for eye health.
🔬 IMA Sleep Breath Meditation: Chrysanthemum contains luteolin with anti-inflammatory effects on ocular tissue. Confidence 6/10
Summer Soother
Mung Bean & Lily Bulb Sweet Soup
Mung Beans 50g · Bai He 15g · Rock Sugar · Water 800ml
A cooling dessert for hot summer nights. Mung beans clear summer-heat in TCM; lily bulb calms the spirit. Serve chilled or at room temperature — never ice-cold, which disrupts sleep digestion.
🔬 IMA Good Sleep: Cooling foods before bed in hot climates can lower core temperature, facilitating sleep onset. Confidence 7/10
Warming
Millet & Pumpkin Calming Congee
Millet 60g · Pumpkin 150g · Red Dates 5 pcs · Water 700ml
Millet is the only alkaline grain — gentle on the stomach. Combined with pumpkin's natural sweetness and red dates for blood nourishment. The perfect 6pm dinner for sensitive stomachs.
🔬 IMA Sleep Breath Meditation: Millet is rich in tryptophan (202mg/100g), a precursor to serotonin and melatonin. Confidence 8/10
Evening Eating Rhythm

The Ideal Pre-Sleep Nutrition Timeline

18:00 · Dinner
Light, easily digestible meal. Aim for 70% fullness. Avoid spicy, greasy, or overly rich foods.
20:30 · Herbal Tea
Suan Zao Ren tea or chamomile. Warm liquid promotes the post-meal core temperature drop that signals sleep.
21:30 · Last Sip
Stop drinking 30+ min before bed. Avoid waking for bathroom trips that fragment sleep architecture.
22:00 · Sleep
With 3-4 hours since dinner, digestion is complete and core temperature is dropping — your body is primed for sleep.
