What is a sleep cycle?

An adult sleep cycle lasts roughly 90 minutes and moves through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM. Deep sleep drives physical recovery and immune repair, while REM supports memory consolidation and emotional regulation. Waking at the end of a cycle — in the lightest phase — feels dramatically easier than being yanked out of deep sleep.

FAQ

Why do I still feel groggy after 8 hours of sleep?

Sleep quality depends on completing full sleep cycles, not just total hours. Waking mid-deep-sleep leaves you groggy even after enough rest. Plan around 90-minute cycles to wake at a cycle boundary.

Should I keep a fixed bedtime or a fixed wake time?

Prioritize a fixed wake time. Your circadian rhythm responds strongly to morning light and consistency; bedtime will naturally shift earlier once wake time is anchored.

How can I fall asleep faster?

Dim lights and avoid screens an hour before bed, keep the bedroom cool and quiet, and build a consistent wind-down routine such as warm baths, soft music, or reading a paper book.
