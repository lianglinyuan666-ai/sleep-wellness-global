🎵 Pink Noise vs White Noise for Sleep

Pink noise (1/f spectrum) may enhance slow-wave sleep more than white noise. Research shows pink noise exposure during sleep increased slow-wave activity by 23% in older adults — potentially slowing age-related memory decline.

🧠 The Neurobiology of Insomnia

Chronic insomnia involves hyperactivity in the default mode network and elevated cortisol at bedtime. The "hyperarousal" model explains why insomniacs feel simultaneously exhausted and wired — a state fMRI studies confirm with increased brain metabolism during sleep attempts.

🫁 4-7-8 Breathing: The Vagal Brake for Sleep

Dr. Andrew Weil's 4-7-8 technique (inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s) activates the vagus nerve's parasympathetic response. Extended exhalation increases heart rate variability (HRV), signaling the body to downshift from "fight-or-flight" to "rest-and-digest."
